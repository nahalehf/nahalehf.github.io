01 / ABOUT

Curiosity across disciplines.

I'm a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, where I work with Prof. Mounya Elhilali in the Laboratory for Computational Audio Perception.

My research asks how the brain forms expectations about sounds, detects changes, and adapts to the world around us. I combine EEG, behavioral and physiological measurements with statistical modeling to study these questions.

I'm also interested in translating signal processing and machine learning into practical technology, including efficient deep learning systems for speech enhancement.
